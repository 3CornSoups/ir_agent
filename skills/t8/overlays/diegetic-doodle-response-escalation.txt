Mechanism: 画内涂鸦｜现实互动逐步绘出隐藏世界
Summary: 用第一视角真实互动作为涂鸦触发器：每次手、宠物或物件完成一个可见动作，新的线描符号就锚定在对应现实表面；符号从单个提示升级为动作回声、隐藏对象和路线，最后整套图形连接成覆盖环境的可读世界。

Recommended beat pattern: `现实第一视角互动 + 首个画内线稿回应 + 连续表面路径 + 至少三类响应图形 + 真实空间与完整绘制世界同框`

Mandatory structural anchors (keep order and causality):
- Establish one embodied observer, one responsive subject and the first surface-bound drawing triggered by visible interaction.
- Use a second real action to extend the drawing rule onto a different local surface or route.
- Reveal one new local figure or motif that visibly inherits an earlier line family.
- End on a wider view where prior symbols connect into one coherent environment-wide drawing layer.

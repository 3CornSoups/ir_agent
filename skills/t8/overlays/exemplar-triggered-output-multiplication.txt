Mechanism: 样本倍增｜机器开启后连续输出同类群体
Summary: 先让一个可辨样本与封闭机器同框，经过一次外部触发后打开独立输出阈值；同类实例从阈值连续出现并在机器周围累积，而原样本始终留在顶部作为身份对照，最终同框证明倍增结果。

Recommended beat pattern: `样本与封闭机器同框 + 一次外部触发 + 独立输出阈值打开 + 同类实例连续输出累积 + 原样本保留为身份对照`

Mandatory structural anchors (keep order and causality):
- Establish one identity exemplar and one closed production device in the same readable frame.
- Use one visible trigger and show a distinct output threshold opening before the first result.
- Repeat one-at-a-time outputs through the same threshold and accumulate them in visible space.
- Hold exemplar, device, open threshold and accumulated outputs together.

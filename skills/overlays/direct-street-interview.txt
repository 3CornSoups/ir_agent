Direct street interview / walk-and-talk — writing methodology only.
Source: T8mars/minimax-h3-prompt-skill-T8 skills/direct-street-interview-video (non-official, user-contributed).

Do not change H3 field names, alignment lines, or <Picture>/<Subject> rules.
Do not mention aspect ratio, resolution, fps, or canvas size.
Do not invent reference assets or dialogue. Use only user-stated or inventory-verified facts.
Do not copy any demonstration person's likeness, wardrobe, exact street, dialogue, or beat timing.

Core mechanism:
One continuous street route turns a short conversation into visible relationship change —
establish distance → one readable social signal → adjust pace/position together → land on a resolved state —
while environmental parallax, moving daylight, and location sound prove continuity.

Story spine (one primary chain):
social premise (approach / recognition / question / compliment / request / compact reveal)
→ stable subject identity (2–3 visual anchors + wardrobe continuity)
→ distance / gaze / pace / gesture change
→ readable ending (permission, pose, object shown, route chosen, reaction, or natural release)

Camera (default):
- One continuous first-person handheld follow: backpedaling or side-walking at conversational distance.
- Prefer a single continuous shot unless the base prompt explicitly asks for 分镜 / cuts.
- Camera motion must follow the subject's walk line, turn, stop, gaze, or gesture — not random shake.

Street / environment:
- Foreground occlusion, background parallax, passing light/shadow, small exposure adaptation.
- Foot contact with the ground; subject must not float over the street.

Dialogue:
- Short turns only; each line finishes before the next speaker begins.
- ~10s: prefer 2–3 short spoken turns; ~15s: at most 4–5 unless the user supplied rapid speech.
- Keep dialogue subordinate to visible action. Copy locked lines verbatim in <d>[Language]…</d>.
- Default to verbal or gestural positioning; add touch only when the user explicitly wants consensual contact.

Sound:
- Location ambience + synchronized footsteps / fabric / action foley first.
- Non-diegetic score: sparse documentary bed (instruments + tempo) unless the base prompt explicitly requires non_diegetic_music = N/A / 无配乐.

Feasibility:
- Do not start a new setup at the video endpoint.
- Remove a shot before compressing dialogue into unnatural speed.
- No 4K / HDR / film-stock / lens-metadata claims unless the user requested them.

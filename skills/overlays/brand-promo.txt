Brand / product / website promo — writing methodology only.

Do not change H3 field names, alignment lines, or <Picture>/<Subject> rules.
Do not mention aspect ratio, resolution, fps, or canvas size.
Do not invent logos, product UI, claims, metrics, or CTAs. Use only user-stated or inventory-verified facts; otherwise mark as concept copy.

Story spines (pick one, keep one primary action per beat):
- SaaS / AI: intent → planning → capability → execution → output → proof → logo
- Physical product: hero reveal → interaction → feature macro → usage context → result → logo
- Service / company: context → process → evidence → outcome → promise → logo
- Image-led brand: authentic imagery → visual motif → benefit → emotional payoff → logo

Beats: about 5–8 for a ~15s clip. Product motion, UI flow, light, or matched edges drive transitions. No fake HUD, unverified metric walls, or decorative text stacks.

Identity assets (logo, packing, official UI, mascot, real person): preserve; never redraw or approximate. Abstract motion layers (gradients, grids, particles, type, charts) are allowed.

On-screen copy and CTA: verbatim confirmed wording only. Language follows the brand/campaign, not the chat language.
Sound: brand-safe instrumental + light UI; N/A if the intent wants silence / ambience only.

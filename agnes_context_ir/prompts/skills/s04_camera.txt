# Skill s04 — Camera motion vocabulary (binding)

A complete camera-motion phrase has three dimensions. Medium amplitude and normal speed are usually omitted.

Motion type (use these English terms):
Zoom In / Zoom Out | Push In / Pull Out | Pan Left / Pan Right |
Truck Left / Truck Right | Tilt Up / Tilt Down | Pedestal Up / Pedestal Down |
Arc Shot | Tracking Shot | Static Shot | Shake Slightly / Shake Strongly |
POV | Roll Clockwise / Roll Counterclockwise

Amplitude: `with small amplitude` | `with large amplitude`
Speed: `at slow speed` | `at fast speed`

Write camera motion as a natural English action inside the shot, not as stacked end-of-sentence labels:
  The camera pushes in with small amplitude at slow speed toward the folded letter.

Rules:
- If the user specified a camera intent (including Chinese terms such as 甩镜 / 缓推 / 跟拍),
  RE-EXPRESS it with this official English vocabulary while preserving the intent.
  Never leave untranslated Chinese camera jargon in the body; never drop the intent.
- If unspecified, use a restrained default: Static Shot, or a small slow push-in.
- Forbid flashy whip-pans, chaotic handheld shake, or random cut spam unless the user asked.

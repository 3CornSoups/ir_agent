# Skill s07 — On-screen text (binding)

Any banner, sign, label, subtitle, or neon text that is actually visible on screen MUST be wrapped in English double quotation marks.
Preserve the original text and punctuation verbatim, without translation.

Example:
  A red neon sign reading "营业中" glows above the doorway.

Do not invent on-screen text the user did not require and the assets do not show.

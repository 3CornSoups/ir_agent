# Skill p03 — RETENTION PLAN (r2va planning)

You MUST emit a `[RETENTION PLAN]` section after assets and subjects are known.

One line per label that will appear in `retention_analysis`, pre-choosing an official marker:

Visible: `fully_preserved` | `partially_preserved` | `attribute_transfer` | `weak_reference`
Audio: `fully_copy` | `partially_copy` | `reference` | `weak_reference`

Example:
  <Subject 1>: fully_preserved — identity and costume retained in Shots 1–3
  <Audio 1>: reference — timbre only for (S1); signal not copied

Do not invent labels absent from the asset inventory / KEY SUBJECTS.
Do not write `(Sx)` inside this plan's marker lines (IDs belong in CAST / dialogue only).

# Skill p01 — CAST planning block (planning rounds)

You MUST emit a `[CAST]` section before inventing or listing dialogue.

Format (one line per vocalizing character):
  (S1) = <short visual anchor> | role: <role> | relation: <relation_word> (S2)
  (S2) = ...

Rules:
- Assign IDs in order of first vocal event in the planned timeline.
- Characters who never speak / sing / voice-over get NO `(Sx)`.
- List dialogue as attributed lines only:
  (S1): "verbatim text in original language"
- If speech is banned → write `dialogue=forbidden` and omit attributed lines.
- If the scene is conversational but no concrete lines exist → invent at most 1–2 short
  situational lines HERE (planning only), each attributed to a cast ID.
- Do not invent major characters absent from the intent / assets.

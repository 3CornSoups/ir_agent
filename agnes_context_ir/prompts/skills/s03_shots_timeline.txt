# Skill s03 — Shots and cut timeline (binding)

- `[Shot 1]` has NO timestamp.
- Later shots MUST begin with a strictly increasing cut time inside the video duration:
  `[Shot N] At MM:SS.mmm, ...`
- Ordinary cuts use exactly one of:
  `the camera cuts to` / `the shot cuts to` / `the shot transitions to` /
  `the shot changes to` / `the shot switches to`
- Cross-dissolve, fade, or wipe only when the user explicitly asks.
- A cut must introduce new information (subject, space, state, viewpoint, or time).
  If only distance or a slight angle changes, write camera motion instead of a cut.

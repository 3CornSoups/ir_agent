# Skill s05 — Speakers, voiceover, and continuity tags (binding)

Subjects who speak, sing, or produce an off-screen human voice use stable IDs `(S1)`, `(S2)`, …
- Group speech/singing: compound ID `(S1,S2)`.
- A speaker keeps the same ID across shots.
- Characters who never vocalize receive no speaker ID.
- Assign IDs once in order of actual vocal events in the target video.

When a speaker first appears, establish identity outside `<d>`:
character type, age, gender, on-screen vs off-screen, pitch, timbre, speaking rate, or accent.
Place identifying phrase, ID, action, and delivery OUTSIDE `<d>`.

Voiceover (exact phrase required):
  The man (S1) says in an off-screen voiceover: <d>[English] …</d> while his lips remain completely closed.

Cross-cut continuity:
- Same line crossing a cut → use `<scenetrans>` at connecting points and state that audio continues
  (`continues seamlessly across the cut` / `carries over from the previous shot` / …).
- Speech truncated by video end → use `<cutoff>`.

Never invent a speaker ID absent from upstream `[CAST]`. Never re-attribute a line from one `(Sx)` to another.

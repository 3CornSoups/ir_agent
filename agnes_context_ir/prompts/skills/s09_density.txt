# Skill s09 — Description density (binding)

Every shot must establish what is visible or audible now:
composition, subject appearance and position, environment and lighting,
actions and state changes, camera movement, current sound,
and (when applicable) the exact moment referenced content appears or takes effect.

Avoid reducing the body to a plot summary or a bare list of reference relationships.

For r2va generation tasks, `detailed_description` is normally 350–500 English words.
Dialogue-dense content prioritizes fitting the complete spoken timeline rather than
mechanically reaching a word count. Video-editing descriptions scale with source complexity.

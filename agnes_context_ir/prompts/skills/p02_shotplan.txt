# Skill p02 — SHOT PLAN planning block (planning rounds)

You MUST emit a `[SHOT PLAN]` section covering the full duration.

Format (one line per shot):
  Shot 1 | cut — | <visual beats> | speakers: (S1) or none
  Shot 2 | cut 00:03.500 | <visual beats> | speakers: (S2)

Rules:
- Shot 1 has no cut time (`cut —`).
- Later shots use `MM:SS.mmm` cut times that strictly increase and stay within the duration hint.
- Prefer camera motion over a cut when only distance / slight angle changes.
- Speakers column must use IDs from `[CAST]` only.
- FL2VA defaults to a single shot unless the user explicitly requires multiple shots.
